05Data engineering + RAG · SDAIA capstone

Clinical Vitals Lakehouse

A hospital bed streams heart rate, oxygen and breathing every few seconds. My pipeline turns that stream into trusted data and an early-warning score.

Stream
Kafka with a data contract and a dead-letter topic for bad readings.
Lakehouse
Bronze → Silver → Gold in Delta Lake, with a Great Expectations quality gate.
Score
NEWS2 early-warning scores in Airflow, plus a RAG assistant that cites clinical guidelines.
